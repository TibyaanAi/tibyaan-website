Privacy Policy

Effective October 5, 2026

Tibyaan is built so that your Quran reading and learning stays yours. Most information never leaves your device; what is shared is kept to a minimum, protected, and never sold. This policy explains exactly what happens to your information.

01 Who We Are

Tibyaan is operated by Tibyaan LLC, a Wyoming, USA company. This policy covers the Tibyaan Quran app (iPhone, iPad and Android), the Tibyaan online services that support it, and this website. We provide Tibyaan free of charge to students, families and casual readers. We do not show ads, and we do not sell or rent personal information.

Questions or requests: privacy@tibyaan.ai.

02 Data That Stays on Your Device

Most of what you do in Tibyaan never leaves your phone or tablet. This includes:

  • Learner profiles you create on the device (name, age group, role)
  • Reading position, bookmarks, notes and reading plans
  • Memorization (hifz) progress, Learn to Read progress, and mistake history
  • Mistakes marked by a helper on the same device, including typed notes and voice notes
  • App settings, including language, accessibility and theme choices

We cannot see this data. You can delete it by deleting a profile or removing the app. If you choose to export a backup, the app creates an encrypted backup file and you decide where to keep it (for example in your own iCloud Drive or Google Drive).

03 Microphone and Speech Features

Some optional features use the microphone, and only after you allow it:

  • Following your recitation highlights where you are on the page. Recognition runs on your device.
  • "Say it" in Learn to Read checks that the app heard the letter or word. It works only with on-device recognition; if your device cannot recognize speech privately, the feature stays off. Nothing is recorded or sent to us.
  • Helpers' voice notes are recorded only when a parent, teacher or other helper taps record, and are stored on the device unless shared through a family circle (below).

Spoken letters in Learn to Read use your device's built-in text-to-speech voice. These features never grade pronunciation automatically; a teacher or parent makes those judgements.

04 Family Circles (End-to-End Encrypted)

A family circle lets a learner's helpers, such as a grandparent or teacher on their own phone, see and add mistake marks, voice notes and "ready to recite" requests. Circles are optional.

  • Everything shared in a circle is end-to-end encrypted on the sending device. Our servers pass it along but cannot read it, including names, notes and audio.
  • To deliver messages, our servers handle technical information: random device identifiers and public keys, circle membership, message sizes and timestamps.
  • A parent or guardian approves every person who joins, after comparing four matching words with them. Anyone can leave, and guardians can remove anyone.
  • Encrypted messages and voice notes are deleted from our servers once every member's device has received them, or after 30 days at most. Circles with no activity for 180 days are deleted.
  • There is no private one-to-one messaging. Everything shared in a circle is visible to the learner's guardians.

05 School Learning Accounts

If a school or teacher invites a learner to a class, the app connects to our school service. To make classes work, we store on our servers:

  • Account details: email address, display name, role (student, teacher or school administrator), and a securely hashed password
  • Class membership, invitations, assignments, attendance and teacher feedback
  • Text a student submits for an assignment, and "ready to recite" requests (surah and ayah numbers only)

We use this data only to provide the school service. Schools and teachers manage their classes; we keep this data until the school or teacher deletes it, or until you ask us to delete an account. Personal reading and memorization progress stays on the device unless a student chooses to submit something to a teacher.

06 Where Our Servers Are

Shared data is stored in the region you choose in the app, and the two regions are kept separate:

  • Europe: servers in Germany (Hetzner). Encrypted backups are stored in the EU (Cloudflare R2, EU jurisdiction).
  • United States: servers in New York (DigitalOcean). Encrypted backups are stored in the US (Cloudflare R2).

We do not copy data between regions. Server backups are encrypted before they leave the server and are kept for up to six months so that we can recover from failures.

07 Content Downloads

When you download or stream Quran recitation audio, translations or other content, your device requests those files from content providers such as EveryAyah.com and Tanzil.net. Like any website, these providers can see your device's IP address and the file requested. We do not send them any personal information.

08 What We Do Not Do

  • We do not show advertising of any kind.
  • We do not include analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs in the app.
  • We do not sell, rent or share personal information for marketing.
  • We do not build profiles of children or anyone else.
  • Our servers do not keep access logs of IP addresses. Addresses are used briefly in memory to limit abuse, and are not stored.

09 Children

Tibyaan is designed for learners of all ages, including young children. Core reading and learning features need no account and no personal information.

  • Profiles for children are created on the device by a parent or guardian.
  • Family circles require a guardian to approve every member, and everything shared in a circle is visible to guardians.
  • School accounts for children are set up by their school or teacher, who is responsible for obtaining any required parental consent.
  • Parents and guardians can review, delete or ask us to delete their child's information at any time by contacting privacy@tibyaan.ai.

10 Service Providers

We use a small number of providers to run Tibyaan. They process data only on our instructions:

  • Hetzner (Germany) and DigitalOcean (USA): server hosting
  • Cloudflare: encrypted backup storage
  • Let's Encrypt: secure (HTTPS) certificates
  • GitHub Pages and Porkbun: hosting and domain name service for this website
  • Apple and Google: app distribution, and on-device speech features provided by your phone's operating system

11 Security

  • All connections to our servers use HTTPS (TLS).
  • Family circle content is end-to-end encrypted; backups are encrypted before upload.
  • Servers accept administrator access only with security keys, run a firewall, and install security updates automatically.

No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach affecting your information, we will notify affected users and authorities as the law requires.

12 Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK, US state laws such as the CCPA, or COPPA for children under 13), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent. To make a request, email privacy@tibyaan.ai. We will respond within the time the law requires and will not treat you differently for exercising your rights. Because much of your data is only on your device or end-to-end encrypted, we may be unable to access it; in those cases you can delete it directly in the app. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

13 This Website

This website is a set of static pages. It has no sign-in, forms, analytics or advertising trackers. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser shares standard request information (such as your IP address) with Google and with our website host when you visit.

14 Changes to This Policy

We will update this policy when our apps or services change, and change the date at the top. If we make significant changes, we will let you know in the app before they take effect.

Questions about your privacy?

We're happy to explain anything in this policy or help with a request.
