Tibyaan for Schools & Academies

Free for families · Simple per-student pricing for schools

Tibyaan is free for families and for individual teachers. Schools and academies that run classes in Tibyaan pay a small amount per student, per year, so that the service can keep running without ads.

01 How It Works

  • Families and individual teachers: free, for up to 10 students.
  • Schools and academies: pay per student, per year.
  • 30-day free trial: every new school starts with 30 days free, with no payment needed to begin.
  • Pay by card or invoice: payments are handled securely by Stripe. We never see full card numbers.
  • Free schools stay free: if your school or masjid class teaches without charging students, email schools@tibyaan.ai and we will set you up at no cost.
  • VAT in the EU and UK: VAT-registered schools can enter their VAT number at checkout. If your school is in the EU or UK and has no VAT number, please email schools@tibyaan.ai before paying.
  • Your data stays in your region: school data is kept in the region the school chose in the app (Europe or the United States). Paying does not move it.

Pricing is per student per year. The exact amount is shown at checkout before you pay, or email schools@tibyaan.ai for a quote.
